Everything you need
to know.

How long does it take to get my first video? +
Upload your photos and Xelo delivers both the MLS and social video in minutes. No editing, no approvals — it's ready to publish.
Which languages does Xelo support? +
9 languages: English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Arabic, Russian, Mandarin, and Swedish. Xelo writes your property description in all 9 languages automatically. James AI responds in whatever language the buyer writes in — even if it's not on this list.
What is Xelo Market and do I have to pay extra for it? +
Xelo Market is included in every plan, including Trial. It's a property portal built around video — every listing comes with a professional video, multilingual copy, and James AI for buyer enquiries. Your listings are published automatically when you create them — no extra upload step.
How does the video time allowance work? +
Each plan includes a monthly video seconds allowance. One 6-second clip costs 6 seconds from that allowance — generating or re-rendering draws from the same pool. Allowances reset on the 1st of each month; unused seconds do not roll over. Trial includes 36 seconds (6 clips). Starter includes 192 seconds (~32 clips). Professional includes 576 seconds (~96 clips). Agency has no cap.
Can I use Xelo on my existing website? +
Yes. The embed widget lets you add a Xelo video player to any website with one line of HTML. Your listings already live on Xelo Market — the embed just mirrors them on your own site. No API key or backend needed.
